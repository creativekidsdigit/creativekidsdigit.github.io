# How to Create a Calm After-School Routine for Kids with ADHD (Free Printable Guide)

After the school bell rings, many families face a rush of homework, snacks, and big emotions. A simple, predictable routine can turn that chaotic transition into a calmer, more connected part of the day. Below is a step-by-step guide you can start using today-no special training needed.

## Why a routine helps
- **Predictability reduces anxiety.** When kids know what comes next, they feel safer and are less likely to melt down.  
- **Visual cues support executive function.** Pictures or icons act as reminders, so kids can follow steps without constant verbal prompting.  
- **Consistency builds independence.** Over time, children learn to start tasks
